# Image credits and licence status

**Important:** every photo in `public/images/menu/` is a web photo found by image search while the site was built. The build environment could not download the owner's own photos from heritagebites.co.za, so these are placeholders. Most are **not licensed for this business**. Before launch, replace them with photos of Heritage Bites' own food. Keep any photo only after confirming its licence on the source page.

**Where to replace:** add the owner's photos to `public/images/menu/` with the same file names, or change the image lists in `lib/seed-data.ts`. The staff console's launch checklist has an item for this.

## Checks before launch

- `tlhakwana-1.webp` came from a search for **pork** trotters. Check that it shows beef trotters, as the menu says, or replace it.
- The Dreamstime stock previews (`motogo-1`, `motogo-2`, `pap-2`, `russian-2`) were removed because they carry a visible watermark. Motogo wa Mabele, Pap and Russian & Chips each show one photo until the owner's photos arrive.
- The Heritage Bites logo is not yet in the build (see the README, "Logo").

## Licence categories

- **Stock or free-photo site** (Dreamstime, Vecteezy, Adobe Stock): the licence varies by file. Confirm before any commercial use.
- **Pexels:** free licence. Confirm on the source page.
- **Recipe blog, news site or Pinterest pin:** copyright belongs to the publisher or photographer. Not licensed for this business. Replace.
